## Required record for every exercise

- English and Arabic name
- Source chapter and page
- Category and OPT phase/level
- Equipment
- Primary and secondary muscles
- Setup and execution steps
- Breathing and coaching cues
- Common errors
- Regression and progression
- Safety notes and contraindications
- Sets, reps, tempo, rest, and frequency where the source specifies them
- Image/video reference, only after source verification
